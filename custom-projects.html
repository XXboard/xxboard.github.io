Custom hardware development projects

We take on electronic hardware development projects.

XXboard helps clients develop electronic products from schematic design and PCB layout to firmware, structure design, SMD prototypes, and PCBA production.

Development Flow

From idea evaluation to PCBA production.

A typical project is divided into clear stages, so requirements, design files, prototype work, and production preparation are easier to confirm.
01

Project evaluation

Confirm product requirements, feature scope, interfaces, cost, schedule, technical risks, and delivery method.

02

Schematic design

Design and confirm power, controller, interfaces, sensors, communication, protection, and related circuit blocks.

03

PCB design

Prepare footprints, placement, routing, mechanical coordination, DRC checks, and manufacturing files.

04

Prototype SMD assembly & debugging

Arrange SMD assembly, prototype soldering, hardware power-up, basic function verification, and issue fixes.

05

Small-batch PCBA trial production

Use small-batch builds to verify production stability, component alternatives, test flow, and assembly issues.

06

Mass PCBA production

Support mass production with documentation, production communication, test suggestions, and issue tracking.
