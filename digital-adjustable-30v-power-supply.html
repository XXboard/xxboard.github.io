Adjustable DC Power

Digital Adjustable 30V DC Power Supply

An open-hardware digitally controlled DC power module with separate voltage and current controls, dual numeric displays, output terminals and a dedicated output switch.

3–30V DCV/I ControlDual DisplayOpen Hardware

3–30V adjustable output

The PCB silkscreen identifies a nominal adjustable output range from 3V to 30V.

Independent V/I controls

Two rotary encoders provide direct adjustment of voltage and current settings.

Dual digital display

Separate three-digit displays present voltage and current information at the same time.

Output control

A dedicated ON pushbutton allows the DC output to be enabled or disabled from the front panel.

Binding-post output

Red and black terminals provide clearly marked positive and negative DC output connections.

Trigger interface

A header marked TRG exposes a trigger or control interface for integration experiments.

Open production files

The project archive includes schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, Gerber data, drill files and mechanical references.

Validation note

Maximum current, output power, regulation accuracy and protection behavior require documented bench validation before being specified.
