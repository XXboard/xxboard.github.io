STM32 MCU Development Platform

STM32F103RCT6 Multimedia Development Board

A completed open-hardware controller board that brings display, touch, storage, communication and sensor interfaces together on one STM32F103RCT6 platform.

STM32F103RCT6LCD & TouchMicroSDUSB & RS232

STM32F103RCT6 core

The 32-bit STM32 microcontroller provides the central processing platform with accessible development and expansion signals.

Display and touch interfaces

Dedicated connections support LCD and resistive-touch development, making the board useful for embedded user-interface projects.

On-board storage

A MicroSD slot, W25Q64 SPI Flash and AT24C02 EEPROM provide removable, high-capacity and non-volatile configuration storage.

USB serial and RS232

CP2102 USB-to-UART and a MAX232-based DB9 interface support both modern USB serial development and traditional RS232 equipment.

RTC and sensors

The design includes a real-time-clock section plus infrared receiver and DS18B20 temperature-sensor connections for practical experiments.

Debugging and expansion

SWD/JTAG, expansion headers, keys and indicator LEDs support firmware development, testing and custom peripheral integration.

Completed hardware

A working physical board has been assembled. The project archive includes schematic, PCB, BOM and Gerber source material.

Design verification

The available PCB design report records zero DRC errors. Public release files are being cleaned and documented before download.
